07 · Double-Entry Explained

The balance scale that keeps books honest

Every transaction has two sides. Debits must equal credits. When they don't balance, something's wrong.

Debits (left side)
Prepaid Tour Costs: $3,451.50
Bank & FX Fees: $147.50
Software Subscriptions: $200.00
Employee Receivable: $113.44
$3,912.44
⚖
Credits (right side)
Cash — Chase Operating: $3,912.44
$3,912.44
✓
Balanced: Debits = Credits

What's happening?

Cash goes down (credit) by $3,912.44. That must be offset by things going up (debits):

  • Prepaid costs (asset): we paid for a future show
  • Expenses (decrease equity): fees and subscriptions consumed now
  • Employee receivable (asset): employee owes us money back

The equation always holds: Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Every entry must balance.
